About

I am a PhD candidate at Macquarie University (Sydney, Australia), supervised by Dr. Ningning Hou. My research focuses on the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT), including LoRa networks and AI agents for IoT systems. Before my PhD, I worked in industry for about six years as a computer vision engineer, mainly on perception for autonomous driving. I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from The University of Adelaide and my B.Eng. degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT).

Research interests: AIoT, LoRa, AI Agents, Computer Vision
