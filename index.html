About

I am a PhD candidate at Macquarie University, advised by Dr. Ningning Hou. I build AI agents that perceive, reason about, and act in the physical world through IoT devices.

My research sits at the intersection of AI agents and the Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT). I study how networked devices can sense their environment reliably, and how LLM-based agents can turn those observations into decisions and actions with little human intervention. My background is in computer vision, particularly perception for autonomous driving.

Research interests: AIoT, LoRa, AI Agents, Computer Vision
